## Policies to approve

- Customer cancellation deadlines and refund amounts; weather/operator cancellation; no-shows; rescheduling and price differences.
- Handling of late payments and unavailable seats; staff authority to approve exceptions.
- Privacy wording, retention period, access/deletion requests and owner contact.
- Safety/eligibility instructions supplied by the operator; medical/waiver collection handled separately unless explicitly added to scope.
- Confirmation, cancellation and reschedule email content; enquiry response expectation.
- Booking lookup on another device: secure recovery or staff-assisted support.
- Approved logo, photos, video, brochure, Instagram and KK Tours relationship.
